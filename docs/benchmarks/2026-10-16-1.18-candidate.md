## Release scope

Negotiated richer tones add individual gain, sine, triangle, square, pulse
and sawtooth waveforms, adjustable pulse width, linear pitch sweeps and
fixed-duration ADSR envelopes with linear or exponential curves. Standalone
and timeline tones share rendering and cancellation. Older wire formats and
Classic defaults remain unchanged. See the [tone plan](../plans/richer-tones.md)
and [protocol](../protocols/presentation-timeline.md#richer-tones-version-6).
