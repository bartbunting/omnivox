## Context

Windows kill-on-close jobs terminate worker trees when the supervisor dies, but
the same failure removes the writer of their cleanup receipts. The successor
therefore remains blocked even after native handles confirm termination. The
[Windows investigation](../benchmarks/2026-10-26-windows-recovery-review.md)
also establishes that retaining a query-only job handle changes kill-on-close
lifetime. An observer cannot be added without accepting explicit ownership and
termination obligations.

## Decision

Use one cleanup guard for the private Windows session's bounded membership of
1–32 isolated lane workers. Run it as a private mode of the selected, pinned
Omnivox executable. It owns worker creation, the existing per-lane unnamed
kill-on-close jobs, actual child handles, native launch gates, native pins and
the original cleanup journal claim. Establish job ownership before native engine
initialization. The guard stays outside all worker jobs.

The configuration supervisor keeps membership, retained startup and recovery
records, asset retention, immutable Commit/Abort decisions and terminal results.
The guard neither selects configuration nor votes in reload. Speech I/O stays in
the supervisor and connects directly to each worker, without a guard forwarding
hop. A bounded private lifecycle channel is independent of speech, client output
and any WSL relay.

Retain the actual supervisor process handle from startup; never acquire lifetime
authority by reopening a saved PID. Restrict inherited handles. Keep launch gates
closed until native ownership is durable and the complete speech-handle handoff
is acknowledged. Partial duplication, cancelled setup and lost acknowledgements
retain their native and I/O owners; cancellation cannot release a late gate.

Supervisor loss closes admission for the entire session. The guard retires every
worker job, including helpers whose worker has already exited, independently of
blocked client I/O or journal writes. Native tree retirement requires the actual
child to end and job accounting to reach zero, not a count of expected PIDs.
Cleanup of one lane must not prevent attempts to retire another.

Tree retirement and I/O retirement remain separate durable observations. While
the supervisor lives, its completion indication follows retirement of every
covered I/O operation and setup task. A signalled retained supervisor process
handle may establish completion only for resources owned by that process. The
guard must separately retire its own handles, setup and I/O tasks, and retain its
journal writer through completion. Only the original owner writes these receipts;
a successor's inspection claim grants no new cleanup authority.

Recovery reconciles retained configuration decisions and waits for complete native
and I/O evidence before starting fresh worker incarnations from the last retained
committed configuration. It neither reattaches to old workers, replays speech nor
rereads subsequently edited configuration files. Guard failure, uncertain handoff,
missing receipts and damaged history keep recovery blocked, preserving pins and
conflicting-management exclusion. Simultaneous guard failure and machine-loss
recovery are outside this availability guarantee.

Keep ADR 0014's Unix contract and existing standalone, remote and managed
validation/Apply paths. This adds no distributable helper, shared daemon, engine
pool or public session selection. Full Windows development staging and native
failure/recovery qualification precede enabling the new path.

## Consequences and alternatives

The extra process preserves cleanup authority across supervisor failure for all
lanes while configuration decisions remain centralized. It adds bootstrap,
handle-transfer and completion accounting, with an unmeasured startup and resource
cost. It does not eliminate per-lane engine memory or guarantee physical audio
latency.

Keeping the writer in the supervisor leaves the demonstrated recovery blocked.
A successor cannot infer cleanup from a free claim, EOF, a saved PID or job name.
A query-only observer still affects job lifetime and needs explicit termination
authority. Per-lane owners add processes without simplifying session decisions.
An external service would add deployment and version-selection requirements.

Exact contracts and acceptance stages belong in the
[reload reference](../reference/session-configuration-reload.md) and
[delivery plan](../plans/session-configuration-reload.md#windows-cleanup-guard-review).
Acceptance is not evidence of implementation, platform qualification or release
publication.
