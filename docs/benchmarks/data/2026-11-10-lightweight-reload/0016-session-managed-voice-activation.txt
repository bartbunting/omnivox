## Context

The existing voice-library Apply path has Emacsvox coordinate two independently
owned speech workers. A private session instead retains one profile lease and
owns 1–32 lanes. The [boundary checks](../benchmarks/2026-10-26-session-library-boundary.md)
show that the live session excludes existing library inspection and Apply. Closing
the session restores inspection but removes the speech pair and actual startup
records expected by the old Apply workflow.

A transition through fresh legacy workers would add another restart, temporarily
remove Reload and require a policy for additional lanes. Centralized management
needs an explicit relationship between the session's committed configuration and
the managed library's active pointer, especially when a crash separates their
writes. Preference-only reload does not establish that relationship.

## Decision

### Keep library coordination in the owning session

Use the session supervisor's retained file task and profile lease for library
inspection, desired-state edits and managed Apply. Keep conflicting external
management excluded. Emacsvox presents review, progress and results and supplies
its frozen client settings; Omnivox controls native preparation, retirement,
replacement, publication and recovery. Installation and desired enablement alone
do not change running speech.

Apply a reviewed managed generation to the complete frozen membership. Preserve
stable lane identities, independent cancellation, per-lane output and acknowledged
client settings. Derive candidates from the session's committed launch context
and selected managed generation. Preserve helper definitions and speech policy;
unrelated configuration-file edits do not enter this operation. Reject conflicting
explicit provider overrides before interruption.

Retain exact old settings and candidate recovery records, reviewed index identity,
the previous active pointer and every worker incarnation. The previous pointer
and the actual running configuration are separate inputs. Rollback restores the
actual settings, even when they differ from the profile's previous pointer.

### Verify candidates under retained native ownership

Keep working speech available during preflight. The existing session cleanup
guard owns at most one additional probe worker at a time, retiring its native
tree and I/O before the next probe. This operation resource does not increase the
32-lane membership limit. Candidate and rollback workers also belong to that
original guard; native admission never implies configuration commitment.

After successful preflight and a final unchanged-input check, close admissions
and retire the old set. Create fresh candidate incarnations and verify actual
configuration, eligibility and client readiness on every lane. Open ordinary
speech only after the complete candidate set and publication are verified.
Keep strict Apply validation even where ordinary startup permits fallback.

### Use one durable decision for publication and recovery

The retained session decision is the commit authority. Persist Commit only after
all candidate lanes pass the required readiness checks. Publish the candidate
active pointer under the held profile lease, retain publication completion, then
release speech admission. These separate writes are not an atomic filesystem
transaction.

After a crash, a verified Commit selects the candidate. If the pointer still has
the exact previous bytes, finish publishing the candidate; if it already has the
exact candidate bytes, verify completion without applying twice. A conclusively
absent Commit with an unchanged previous pointer permits Abort. Unexpected
pointer bytes, damaged history or uncertain writes keep recovery blocked.
A missing reply or terminal result cannot reverse Commit or invent readiness.

A successor must establish that the original writer is gone and verify cleanup
of all original, probe, candidate and rollback trees and I/O before completing
pointer publication or admitting fresh speech. Retain asset references and
management exclusion until the relevant evidence permits release. Never infer
cleanup from elapsed time, a free lock or recorded PIDs.

Cancellation before retirement preserves old speech after probe cleanup. Failure
or cancellation after retirement but before Commit restores the exact old settings
where proven; failed rollback remains explicitly unavailable. Client loss closes
the session, and later explicit recovery uses the retained selection. After
Commit, recovery moves forward. Never reread edited files for recovery or replay
interrupted speech. Machine-loss and damaged-history recovery have no forced
repair path.

### Preserve existing boundaries

Use distinct bounded records and negotiated operations. Preserve old history and
the standalone, old-client and remote contracts. Older executables must reject
unsupported history before native launch. Arbitrary helper/output configuration
Apply, shared hosting and remote management remain outside this decision.

Qualify all existing library actions before ordinary session adoption, including
validation, import and removal. Routing them through the owning session must
retain their existing asset checks, package references and deletion rules. A
working Apply alone does not qualify the whole library workflow.

## Consequences and alternatives

One native coordinator preserves all lanes and gives the running session and
saved voice selection one recovery authority. The cost is new activation history,
guard admissions and client readiness integration. Sequential preflight bounds
extra engine residency but can take longer for large memberships.

A bridge to the old two-owner workflow adds a temporary lifecycle and extra
restarts. Relaxing the profile lease would allow conflicting management without
settling publication or rollback. Deferring integration keeps private sessions
experimental and blocks their ordinary adoption.

The [delivery plan](../plans/session-configuration-reload.md#managed-voice-library-integration-review)
contains the sequence and acceptance requirements. Exact formats belong in the
references and observed results in the evidence archive. Acceptance establishes
no working public Apply capability, platform qualification or release availability.
